Compile an AR image target

Turns your printed artwork into the .mind file that card.html tracks. Everything runs in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and it costs nothing.

Drop your card or pamphlet artwork here PNG, JPG or SVG · one file per printed design

What makes a target track well

  • Busy beats beautiful. The tracker looks for corners. A logo on white has almost none; a printed floor plan with walls, dimension ticks and text has thousands.
  • Fill the whole card. Artwork covering 100% of the surface tracks far better than a small mark in the middle.
  • Asymmetric. A design that looks the same rotated 180° will make the model flip.
  • No repeats. Tiled or striped patterns confuse matching.
  • Print matte. Gloss lamination reflects ceiling lights straight into the lens and tracking dies.
  • Bigger is steadier. An A5 pamphlet holds far better than an 89 × 51 mm visiting card. Test both.

Ready-made designs to print are in public/ar/print/. Compiling one 1024 px target takes a few seconds on a laptop with working WebGL, and several minutes without it.
